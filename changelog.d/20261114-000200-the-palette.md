**The palette replaces the launcher**: the key super opens one panel that lists the programs and the windows in one ranked list, with the fuzzy match of the console and each matched character marked. Enter starts the chosen program or brings the chosen window to the front; the close of a window asks for a second Enter. The input band is the top of the panel, flush with its top, left and right edges, and the typed part is at twice the size of the font. The pointer chooses and runs a row too. The field `launcher` of `compositor.pointer()` is now `palette`, the state of the panel as a number, and the hint of the bar is "palette: super".

**The mark has two variants, and one command draws any picture of it**: `kerneltool mark` writes the still mark, a frame of an animation, a tile map or its table, in the smooth variant (each pixel takes the gradient at its own place) or in the cells variant (each cell is one color), at a cell size and in a color scheme that the command names. The head of the home of the site keeps the smooth variant with its real pixels; the bar and the icon of a tab are the cells variant, one pixel for a cell. The files of the mark of the repository and of the site are now one table, which `kerneltool mark uses` prints, and each PNG file of the mark is written at the smallest size that loses no pixel. A request with no sense, as the smooth variant at one pixel for a cell, is refused with a sentence.

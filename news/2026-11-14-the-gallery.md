The site has a page named Gallery. It shows 109 pictures of the screen of the system, in 11 groups: the desktop and the palette, the console, the mark, the rights and the faults, the modules, the memory and the threads, the developer tools, the fonts, the network, the storage and the boot. Each picture has a caption of one sentence that says what it shows, and a link to the guide of its subject. A picture on the page is at one half of its size. Its link opens it at its full size, where one pixel of the picture is one pixel of the screen of the system.

The home now shows 8 of these pictures in one row, one picture in view. A numbered link above the row shows each picture, and the row also scrolls sideways. The row never changes its picture by itself. The picture of the program `heron` is one of the 8.

The pictures and their captions come from one table. A new picture of a machine test that the table does not name stops the build of the site, so no picture is left out.

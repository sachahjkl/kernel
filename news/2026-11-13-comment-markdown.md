The reference pages of the site come from the comments of the code, and those comments are now Markdown: bold, italics, lists, examples in code and links. Each kernel operation has its arguments, its results and its statuses as lists, with the rights that it needs in bold and links to its records and to its guide. A link names a place of the site: an operation, an export of a module, a declaration with its card, a term or a heading of a guide. A link that names nothing stops the build of the site. In the running system, `manual` shows the same text as plain lines.

The system has a mark: a heron of square cells on a grid of 24 by 24, each part filled with one gradient, and the name modus under it. The mark is data in `support/mark`, and `kerneltool logo` writes its files in `resources/logo`, on a dark and on a light background. The file of the mark at 1024 by 1024 pixels has 16 KiB with its compression, in place of 3 MiB with none.

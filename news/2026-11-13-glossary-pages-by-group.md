title: The glossary has a page for each group
date: 2026-11-13
release: 0.92.0
picture: none
kind: Decided
guide: none

The glossary of the site is now one page for each group of terms, as Code, Access or Network, and an index. The single page had 1 301 073 bytes once each of its 460 terms had its explanation. No page of the glossary is larger than 1 MB now. The index lists every term from A to Z, with its group and its short definition. A link to a term on any page opens the page of its group. An address of an entry at the index still works: the index sends it to the page of the group.

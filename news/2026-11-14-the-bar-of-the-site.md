The site has six sections, and six buttons do not fit the one line of its bar on a small window. The bar now has three buttons: Docs, Gallery and News. They are what a first reader looks for: what the system is, what it looks like, and what is new. A fourth button, Project, opens a small panel under it with three more pages: Status, Releases and Roadmap. They report how the project goes. The pictures of the system are one click from each page. The panel opens and closes with no script. On a phone, the menu of the site lists the six sections one under the other.

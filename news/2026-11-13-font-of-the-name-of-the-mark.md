The letters of the name of the mark are now a font file, `resources/logo/modus_mark.ttf`. Its glyphs are the square cells of the five letters of "modus", from the same table as the pictures of the mark: each letter is one outline around its cells. One em is 8 cells, so a text at 16 pixels has cells of 2 pixels. The site writes the name of the system in this font in its bar, in its foot and in the title of the home. The name is text there: a reader can select it, and a screen reader says it. The pictures of the mark on a page now show the heron alone. The heron of the home also moves more often: an animation starts each 2.4 seconds on average, in place of each 4.7 seconds. Nothing moves for a reader who asks for reduced motion.

The site has a new page, Roadmap, beside the page Releases. The page Releases lists the work that is done. The page Roadmap lists the work that is planned. It has three parts. Now is the work that is in progress. Next is the work that is decided and comes after. Later is the work that is wanted and has no order. Each entry is a card with a short summary and one or two sentences that say what the work gives a user of the system. An entry gives no date. When a work is done, its entry leaves the page, and the work is a change of a release. The search of the site finds each entry by its summary. The page is made from one file of the repository, `ROADMAP.md`, which a reader understands with no tool.

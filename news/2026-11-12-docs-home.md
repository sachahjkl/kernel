The site has one section Docs. Its first page has a search field over the whole site, the list of every guide with one line that says what it holds, and the list of every reference table with its count. A chart shows how many entries of each kind the search can find. While a person types, the chart shows how many entries of each kind match, and a press on a row keeps the results to that kind. Each old address of the site leads to its new one.

date: 2026-11-14

The home of the site has a line of time of the start of the machine. One bar shows the whole start on one scale: the firmware takes most of it. A second bar shows the stages of the boot program and of the kernel on a larger scale, and a sentence says how much larger. Each stage has a row with its name and its time. Point at a stage, or give it the focus, and a card says what the stage does, which code runs it, and where its guide is. The measures of the home are now charts: bars of one unit, from zero. A measure that is not such a chart is on the page of the measured numbers. The newest release shows its number, its counts and its first changes. A press on the heron plays an animation at once. Each button of the site now shows the pointer, the focus and a press, in the colors of the buttons of the desktop.
